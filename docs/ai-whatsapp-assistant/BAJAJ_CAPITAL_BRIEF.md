## 0. One-page summary

**Who they are.** Bajaj Capital Ltd was founded in 1964 in New Delhi by K.K. Bajaj and incorporated in 1965 [1][5]. It calls itself "India's premier investment services group engaged inter-alia in the distribution of mutual funds, insurance, fixed deposits, bonds, loans et al." [1]. It is an AMFI-registered mutual fund distributor (**ARN 0010**). Its disclaimer says it may give *incidental* advice on mutual funds only to its MF distribution clients [8]. Its website says it serves **over 4.1 million clients** [2].

**Who you are likely meeting.** Confirm which "CEO" the meeting is with:
- **Jai Bajaj**, CEO & Managing Director since **1 April 2026** (announced 23–24 June 2026) [2][14][15]. He is third generation, founded **RTR (Ready to Retire)**, and his stated focus is "technology and AI, advisor productivity, geographical expansion, and moving from a distribution-led model to one built around client excellence" [2].
- **Samir Dewan**, Group Chief Executive Officer (reported 28 Sep 2026). He came from IndusInd Bank, RBC and ABN AMRO, and his mandate is integration across the group [6][17][18].

**Why the timing is good.**
- The firm is publicly investing in "AI-powered intelligence" while keeping advisors at the centre [15][16]. It has appointed a Head of Data Science [14][16].
- It already publishes a WhatsApp chat number [1][7], and its new "One" app offers WhatsApp notifications [13].
- It runs a workplace programme covering 250+ corporates [20] and launched Global Markets at GIFT City in September 2026 [19].

**Our ask (recommendation).** A **6-week pilot** on two journeys, each with a measurable KPI agreed upfront:
1. Acquisition: a retirement-readiness ("RTR") chat that ends in an advisor booking.
2. Retention: two-way insurance-renewal or SIP-failure conversations.

**Biggest sensitivities.**
1. As an MFD, Bajaj Capital may only give incidental advice. The assistant must educate and route to advisors, never recommend.
2. The insurance broking licence rules on solicitation apply.
3. SEBI makes regulated entities answerable for AI outputs.
4. The DPDP Act applies to the personal data collected.
5. Meta's ban on general-purpose AI bots on WhatsApp means the assistant must stay scoped to the business.

### A9. Regulatory facts relevant to the pitch (verified)

- SEBI's Intermediaries (Amendment) Regulations, 2025 were notified in Feb 2025. Any SEBI-regulated person using AI/ML tools is solely responsible for the outputs, for investor-data privacy and security, and for compliance, whether the tool is built in-house or bought [27].
- Meta's WhatsApp Business Solution terms (for all users from 15 Jan 2026) bar **general-purpose** AI assistants. Coverage indicates that business-specific support and sales bots remain allowed [25].
- Since 1 Oct 2025, SEBI-registered intermediaries that collect investor money use "@valid" UPI handles, and investors can check payment details with "SEBI Check" [28].
- SEBI's MF categorisation was revised on 26 Feb 2026. Solution-oriented (retirement/children's) schemes were discontinued and Life Cycle Funds introduced [30]. SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 2026 apply from 1 Apr 2026; reg. 44(4) caps the exit load at 3% of NAV [31].
- The Income-tax Act, 2025 applies from 1 Apr 2026. Old section 80C is now **section 123**, 80CCD is **124**, 80D is **126** and 87A is **156** [29]. The tax year 2026-27 slabs are unchanged [32]. *This renumbering will confuse clients in the Jan–Mar tax season, and that confusion is a campaign opportunity.*

### B2. Tailored pitch angles

1. **"Advisor amplifier, not advisor replacement."** This mirrors their own words about technology that enhances advisors with "human expertise" at the centre, and Jai Bajaj's *advisor productivity* focus. Advisors receive pre-qualified leads with goals, risk profile and full context.
2. **"From distribution-led to client excellence."** Use his phrase. The assistant makes every client touchpoint a planning conversation: goals, reviews, renewals.
3. **"RTR, now on WhatsApp."** This connects his own venture (RTR), Rajiv Bajaj's longevity and "Retirement Project" themes, and the June 2026 promise of digital initiatives on financial readiness and retirement.
4. **"One front door for the group."** This fits Samir Dewan's integration mandate: one WhatsApp number that routes MF, insurance, NPS, Global Markets and La Premiér to the right desk.
5. **"Compliance by design."** Every answer is sourced from an approved knowledge base and traced (tools used, documents cited, guardrails fired). It adds disclaimers automatically, redacts PII (PAN/Aadhaar), supports STOP/opt-out, and lets a human take over live. This matches SEBI's 2025 accountability rule for AI tools.
6. **"Data that feeds data science."** Structured intents, goals and lead scores flow to the new Head of Data Science for personalisation.
7. **"Tax season is coming, and the law changed."** The Income-tax Act, 2025 renumbering (80C is now 123) creates confusion that a sourced, always-on explainer can turn into bookings.
8. **"We run it ourselves."** The same engine powers Assured Rewards' own customer support (the `assured-rewards` tenant), so it is not a slideware demo.

### B3. Suggested demo script (5–7 minutes)

1. **Simulator, Hinglish:** "SIP kya hai?" The answer comes with sources and quick-reply buttons.
2. "Salary 15 lakh hai, tax kaise bachaun?" The bot gives an illustrative old vs new regime comparison and cites sections 123/124/126 of the new Act.
3. "Age 35, kharcha 50k/month, retirement plan?" The corpus and SIP calculator runs, clearly labelled *illustrative*.
4. A short risk-profile quiz, then "Talk to an advisor": real slots, then a booking confirmation card.
5. **Console:** live conversation, lead score and stage, trace (tools, KB hits with source URLs). **Take over** as a human advisor and reply.
6. **Guardrails:** paste a fake PAN to show redaction. Ask "guaranteed 20% return?" to show a refusal plus fraud education (@valid UPI, never share OTP). Ask "Is this official Bajaj Capital?" to show the concept-demo disclaimer.
7. **Resilience:** show the rule-based fallback answering FAQs if the LLM is unavailable.

> Branding caution: the demo tenant uses Bajaj Capital's name and colours as a *concept*. Keep it on the simulator or a private test number. Don't connect it to any public WhatsApp number with Bajaj Capital branding until they agree in writing.
